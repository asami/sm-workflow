# Scope downstream calculation

## R1 — calculate once and retain the entire frontier

Given a genuinely observed ALL-Built scope publication at its exact unsuspended
postimage, When owning advance calculates and publishes the downstream, Then
admission/objective and every candidate are stored with the full prior scope
and one canonical managed native append. A later call or physical reopen copies
the complete calculation, reports zero newly absorbed stages and performs no
enumeration, allocation, projection, admission/ranking or objective calculation.

## R2 — own the native transaction and observe failures

Given actual owning-call storage grants and original native provenance, When
application Create and native receipt commit, Then they share the canonical
managed transaction. A collision leaves both uncommitted. Unknown commit or
readback reports indeterminate and is recovered only by later observation.
Absent/corrupt/one-sided/later-prefix state, foreign actor or another Operation
cannot grant fresh publication or silently recalculate.

## R3 — genuine reasoning origin only

Given a genuinely observed scope-planning or Human downstream whose complete
saved disposition requires reasoning, When that origin enters reasoning
restoration, Then only the original unknown demand is requested and every known
fact, candidate, closure and authority is preserved. Other dispositions and
passive decoded data grant no reasoning issue, mutation or suspension permission.

This specification is saved calculation/publication, not complete reasoning
issue/completion, public successful advance, document/apply or typed Terminal.
All four origins and full PHASE-2 acceptance remain separate mandatory evidence.

## R4 — public ALL-Built continuation and selected output

Given an actual scope result from either original planning root, complete cost,
demand and overhead records and the paired public continuation profile, When
advance resumes the ALL-Built frontier, Then it publishes the whole downstream
calculation and continues it rather than manufacturing Human work. Known work
requires no AI task. Unknown reasoning classification issues only that unknown
item, and its assigned public result continues the saved scope-planning origin.

An actual Human ProvideResolution answer is independently verified first. Its
referenced interpretation data is supplied separately, not invented by login or
the semantic scope result. Public advance publishes the genuine Human-resolution
successor and continues its selected calculation or unknown-only reasoning task.
Retained completion and physical reopen must not renew Human consent.

The selected plan reaches useful PREVIEW with the original ordered work and
authority. Owning Operation and launcher return the actual native Terminal;
physical reopen without allocation policies returns the same Terminal without
another append, AI call or Human verifier call. This does not establish APPLY,
default installation or the complete three-workflow practical boundary.

E3 covers both original roots, scope-only or independently answered Human
resolution, and known/unknown reasoning through real provided operations with a
test-owned SQLite host. The host's independent Human verifier is called once
only for an actual Human answer and never for ALL-Built scope-only continuation.
Source authoring is not a test PASS; the admitted runner outcome is recorded
separately.
