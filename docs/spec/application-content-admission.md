# Application content admission

sm-workflow consumes complete Git, File and execution results admitted by CNCF.
It does not reject a successful complete capture, freeform Human rationale,
semantic proposal or completion record using another application byte or
character ceiling. Resource protection belongs to CNCF execution and resource
configuration, including `textus.resource.read.max-bytes` for File/resource
reads. Limits are enforced during acquisition, not after a workflow effect.

The application retains its business and integrity checks: required values,
ordinary UTF-8 and NUL rules, capture byte counts and truncation, exact assigned
paths and ordered identities, ownership, selected effects and expected-current
CAS. A real failure, UNKNOWN or uncertain effect remains recorded and never
becomes success or permission to replay. Removing content ceilings does not
change Session history, approvals or repair counts.

Presentation previews may be shorter when complete originals remain retained
and retrievable. Preview limits do not become semantic input limits or claim
full review coverage. Collection cardinalities and typed identifier/path
contracts remain separate business constraints.
