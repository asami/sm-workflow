Planning mappings, Context indices and partition-completion frames are inert
stored data. Their v1 schemas remain supported. Reading a frame does not construct
an executable Plan, current managed call, permission or native publication receipt.

## Raw boundary

Mapping and index readers inspect declared members and nested reference/origin
structure directly, then decode typed values. They do not encode another payload
and compare it with the input JSON. Embedded frame codecs own their declared data.

The partition-completion reader inspects its envelope/schema, all declared payload
members and reference structure, including declared optional fields. Mapping
reference and checkpoint must be paired. Nested frames decode through their own
readers and the actual selected continuation resolves the result. The reader does
not re-encode the complete record or compare regenerated bytes with stored bytes.
Formatting of the outer JSON and a valid nested Context does not gate reopening.

Missing required fields, unsupported variants/schema, malformed types,
crossed selected addresses and the UTF-8 bound remain actual raw-input failures.
No hash, full-copy witness, source-line origin or revision-as-no-change certificate
replaces the removed representation proofs.

### Inert partition-completion capture and codec

compatibility header to compare with itself, or decode its own response to prove
an unchanged lineage. Encoding and existing-v1 decoding preserve typed inert
Record data; neither grants native completion, current authority or publication.
The common complete-record inspector and every caller are removed, not renamed
or moved into a replacement verifier.

The outer v1 codec ignores unused root, payload and reference members. Missing
optional header data decodes as None, not proof of execution or failure of the
whole representation. Actually decoded nested types, selected continuation,
required data, mapping lookup pairing and managed payload byte limits retain
their reader contracts. Native Operation owners still enforce real result,
