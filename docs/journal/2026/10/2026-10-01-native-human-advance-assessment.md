## Implemented boundary

`GoalPhaseNativeHumanAdvanceOperation.bindC` now requires configured creation,
selection, producer artifact, time, snapshot, authority and event dependencies.
It delegates to `GoalPhaseReviewRecoveryServiceOperation.bindNativeAdvanceC`.
That entry uses the same retained-review execution body as native recovery,
but preserves the generated advance Operation and its bounded closed decoder.
The private Factory-selected entry flag is never a request field or Skill
decision. The configured host-record Factory supplies its resolved Blob
snapshot and managed authority readers to both entries.

At revision three, current selection/participant admission precedes the shared
accepted-review projection. That projection admits the exact retained result,
issued review and current candidate, assesses bytes and saved authorization,
rechecks them and the current predecessor, then permits publication only for
Missing authority. Recorded information remains non-executable; stale or
malformed authority refuses. At revision four the common body admits the exact
adjacent pending successor and observes the retained question, without fresh
snapshot/authority assessment or semantic-review replay. Later revision five
cannot be treated as a pending-question recovery.

No new CML Operation, schema, storage collection, permission, execution effect
or human issuer is introduced. Default automatic follow-up remains StopOnly;
manual native advance is an explicit Operation, not a default activation of
automatic follow-up. Legacy stop-only/preparation entries retain their existing
decoders and follow-up path. The private question preparation adapter remains
an internal seam; public advance no longer directly calls that unassessed seam.
