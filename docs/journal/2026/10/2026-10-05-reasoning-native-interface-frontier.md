The next CML increment must declare a reasoning constituent and its own Judgment,
Admission, required capability and public completion Operation before binding an
external participant. Proposed names are `AssessReasoningDemand`,
`AdmitReasoningDemand`, `assessReasoningDemand`, `admitReasoningDemand` and
`submitReasoningResult`, with distinct `SplitPhaseReasoningSemanticRequest` and
`SplitPhaseReasoningSemanticResult` payloads. They are planned names, not current

version; saved v8 instances must not be silently rebound to that new definition.

The request carries selected context/source/authority/baseline demand and the
exact ordered unresolved items. Each item contains its original identity, reason
and complete closure: criteria, prerequisite items and original authority. The
participant returns only `Enriched` classifications
(`ExpensiveReasoning`/`SettledExecution`) with per-item rationale and evidence, or
`DecisionNeeded` with rationale/evidence and no partial assignments. It does not
select replacement data references, policies, costs, winners, stage routing,
actors, host bindings, commands or Terminal. Workflow owns all those decisions.

continuation/task and assigned participant. The application codec has a distinct
schema/type for reasoning; partition or scope responses cannot be substituted.
Decoding is a wire contract, not a file immutability proof or a management hash.

only objective selection over the original constructed frontier. It must not

## Managed continuation and publication

Native issue, canonical completion and the resulting planning checkpoint remain
separate boundaries. The current first checkpoint and Human successor are not
overwritten to imitate a reasoning result. A purpose-specific managed successor
must retain original source/authority/native provenance and the complete
candidate frontier, together with the admitted unknown-only update and objective
outcome. It borrows the owning call's existing Subsystem-managed DataStoreSpace
and pool; no direct SQLite or component-private physical provider is introduced.

On later calls or process reopen, the owner observes actual canonical issue,
completion and committed successor before choosing fresh or retained routing.
Already-known classifications cannot cause another AI task or repeated planner
work. Unknown commit outcome requires observation, not automatic resubmission.
DecisionNeeded or an authority-unequal final tie must lead to a genuine configured
Human interaction; semantic data itself is not consent. Publication followed by
a placeholder success, WAIT or Terminal is not accepted as public continuation.
