## Boundary

The parent continues the user-directed limited recovery without agents. This
is implementation, not Phase acceptance or release. No new product root,
production verifier, Git permission or public transport is enabled.

`GoalPhaseNativeHumanAnswer.prepareC` recovers the exact native Decision,
checks its declared choice and CNCF typed result correlation, and requires the
current Active HumanPending suspension, exact revision, snapshot, completion
and evidence contracts. Its adjacent successor is Active HumanAnswered with
the suspension removed and all predecessor history retained. It does not
close the Step, persist a record or execute a command.

`admitForCallC` separates the independent host human-verifier boundary from
this pure calculation. It binds the owning Component instance, exact question,
answer and submitting participant, and checks the admitted receipt against the
same call. The managed completion caller must still obtain the actual issued
and current records under its captured grants/lease and atomically persist the
completion. No production verifier or native completion writer is connected
by this change.
