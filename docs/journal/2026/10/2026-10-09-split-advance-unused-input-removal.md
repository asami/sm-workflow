## Predicate accounting and limits of this correction

Malformed JSON, a nonobject envelope, missing/wrongly typed consumed fields and
an incompatible schema cannot supply this version's typed instance/context;
conversion must fail rather than guess a target. Those natural failures are
covered separately from unused members. A foreign actual context must not
operate on the original instance: the owning managed context lookup and its
Start/native relationship prevent that wrong effect. The new connected case
distinguishes the two conditions instead of certifying a saved input copy.

The existing 16KiB UTF-8 frame limit and shared physical-locator check remain
unchanged in this increment. Their presence is NOT proof that they or repeated
owning-call decoding are necessary at every current location. Review of those
actual parsing/storage consumption boundaries is still open under Phase5;
this record does not grant a blanket security/validation exemption. Session-
resolved targeting, all other unnecessary checks and the actual AI executor/
completion connection are also still incomplete. None is replaced by this
