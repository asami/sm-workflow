## Authorized direction

Keep the actual current configuration as standard. Provide opt-in cost and quality
profiles through the existing logical profile mechanism. Cost should support
Terra for bounded implementation, repair and required lightweight review, and Luna
for small edits, extraction/classification and bounded judgment. Retain Sol for
complex work; do not change the parent or add AI calls solely for classification.
These are comparison hypotheses, not a declaration of model superiority.

The existing M1/M2 authored selection/capture route and ongoing M3 endpoint work
must be preserved. Do not replace native profile mappings with a new model table,
reconfigure issued work, erase notes, reset counters or rerun accepted tests.
Source documentation changes do not establish installed availability or admission.

## Evaluation contract

Compare equivalent roles/work classes under the same acceptance conditions,
test scope, review triggers and repair limit. Record cohort unit/window, profile
and mapping revisions, configured versus observed model/effort and sample size.
Total consumption includes acquired attributable parent/subagent/review/repair,
retry and escalation usage, including failures, without double-counting inclusive
receipts. Keep Codex/local separate; incomplete observations are not total cost.
Completion wall time includes validation and waiting as well as model execution;
retain existing breakdowns when available. Unfinished/failed work has elapsed time.

Eventual success = accepted completed / all terminal work units in the declared
cohort. First-pass success uses the same denominator and requires no repair/retry.
Retain failed, exhausted, cancelled and deferred outcome counts and show pending
work separately. Do not pool unlike Task/Step/Phase units or attribute mixed-profile
success entirely to the final worker. No measurement is a new progression gate.

The 100:20:10:1 published API unit-price ratio does not establish task cost,
subscription allowance consumption or speed. Prefer measured attributable usage;
label any tariff-based monetary estimate separately with its dated basis.
