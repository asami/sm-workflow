# Phase 8: proportionate validation and bounded closure

Date: 2026-11-11 (Asia/Tokyo)
Status: adopted development direction; implementation and qualification pending
Owner: Phase 8 operational improvement, through existing workflow/effect owners

## Observation and decision

The user observed that repeated limited repairs during Phase 6.11 brought a
large validation and delivery process each time, making the operation inefficient.
Inspection of the repair material distinguished generic defects, contract
extensions and rescue of a particular retained Session state. Even a small source
change could carry a separate Task, review, publication and installed old-to-new
qualification; qualification failures could in turn require more repairs.
This record is an investigation/decision summary, not raw execution output or
an assertion that every past check was unnecessary.

The user selected these Phase 8 requirements:

- Select verification by actual impact/risk and reuse applicable successful
  evidence, preserving required quality and independent review boundaries.
- Group compatible connected repairs for integration/publication/installed
  qualification rather than imposing a full process on every small edit.
- Bound existing-Session rescue using existing accounting and genuine user
  direction; do not recursively grow repair Tasks to repair qualification.
- Keep closure at the adopted acceptance boundary. Record separate findings
  separately; a real blocker still needs resolution or an explicit public
  defer/scope/termination decision, never a manufactured PASS.
