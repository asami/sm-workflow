# Phase 25.4 Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Use the installed Phase 25.3 route during actual Phase 25.4 development.
  Record observed review and compiler-repair selections, actual usage and
  billing evidence when available, and causes for bypass or fallback. Keep
  unobserved cases explicit. Use the resulting evidence to decide when to
  start Phase 25.5.
- [ ] P254-01: Typed multidimensional task taxonomy covers ordinary repair, review and judgment; unknowns and reasoning versus change scope are distinct.
- [ ] P254-02: Context sufficiency, missing evidence and candidate identity recorded; bounded context requests and Take Over verified.
- [ ] P254-03: Provider-neutral capability requirements and deterministic,
  quota-safe strategic judgement routing verified.
- [ ] P254-04: Failure-specific remedies preserve retry limits, recovery, authorization, rollback and Admission.
- [ ] P254-05: Classification and execution observations retain provenance and missingness; comparable qualification evidence distinguishes measured outcomes from hypotheses.
- [ ] P254-06: Verify deterministic resolution with zero AI calls, an
  applicable template sent directly to Terra with zero design-model calls,
  and strategic directive design for a case without an adequate template.
  Verify Terra as the normal executor and Sol selection when an ordinary
  template or AI-designed directive requires its capabilities.
- [ ] P254-06: Qualify the Phase 25.5 connection with a diagnostic strategy
  fixture. Bind an applicable, qualified strategy to a new candidate with the
  same pattern and verify it without a design-model call. Cover ambiguous or
  stale evidence and preserve current-candidate verification and the original
  result's provenance.
- [ ] P254-06: When design is unavailable or unusable, verify selection of an
  applicable prepared directive with zero further design-model calls. Exercise
  Terra and Sol execution according to the directive's capability requirements
  and eligible routes. Verify that an unsuitable directive leaves the issue
  unresolved and that a failed action is not replayed.
- [ ] P254-06: A bounded Strategy Context produces the executor's typed
  directive with review viewpoints or repair instructions. Verify priority
  for verified free Astra, then subscription Sol below the configured weekly
  utilization ceiling, then a prepared directive. Cover ineligible, exhausted
  and unknown free allowance, shared weekly utilization below and at the
  ceiling, stale or unavailable weekly utilization, and reset.
- [ ] P254-06: Verify invalid results, uncertain dispatch or usage, restored
  free allowance, and unchanged mandatory validation and Admission.
- [ ] P254-06: Reusable design artifacts retain provenance and applicability; end-to-end comparison includes design cost, quality, retries and latency.
- [ ] P254-07: Design/execution contracts are independent of entitlement.
  Verify an explicitly selected metered Sol route against its own monetary
  budget, including missing authorization and insufficient funds. Weekly
  subscription utilization cannot admit a metered request.
- [ ] P254-07: Comparable evidence includes total cost per accepted task, quality, latency, failed attempts and reusable design amortization; observed and estimated costs distinguished.
- [ ] P254-08: Human selects the versioned profile before dispatch; ordinary
  behavior is preserved when unspecified. Child Steps and resume retain the
  binding; no AI or allowance change can switch the profile. Verify effective
  mechanisms and unchanged mandatory checks, capability, Admission, retry
  and spending constraints across profiles.
- [ ] Ordinary installed routes and resume qualified; scoped validation and independent review recorded before closure.
